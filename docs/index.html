Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Got questions about Flow Video Merger? Find all the answers below.

What is Flow Video Merger?
Flow Video Merger is an open-source Google Chrome extension (Manifest V3) and Fullscreen Web Studio designed specifically for creators using Google Flow (flow.google) and Google Veo / Veo 2. It automatically collects your generation cards, identifies 4K /boost upscaled videos, organizes your clips with narrative logic, and stitches them into a unified 4K film.
Where can I download the source code on GitHub?
The project is 100% free and open-source under the MIT License on GitHub at https://github.com/leonbathie/flow-video-merger. You can download the latest ZIP directly, star the project, or contribute pull requests.
How does the extension resolve Google Cloud playback errors?
Google Cloud storage video buckets enforce strict Cross-Origin Read Blocking (CORP), often causing net::ERR_BLOCKED_BY_RESPONSE.NotSameSite. Flow Video Merger includes pre-configured declarativeNetRequest rules (rules.json) that safely modify cross-origin response headers so videos stream fluidly in Chrome.
How does the 4K /boost automatic upgrade work?
Whenever you upscale a video in Google Flow, Google renders a 4K version at a /boost storage path. Flow Video Merger detects these URLs and in-place replaces low-resolution draft clips with the 4K master files without disrupting your timeline order.
Is my data uploaded to any third-party server?
Never. Flow Video Merger runs 100% locally on your computer inside Google Chrome. No telemetry, no external databases, no analytics, and no accounts. Your prompts and videos remain strictly private on your machine.
